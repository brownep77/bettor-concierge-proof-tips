What this is

A wider desk. Named people only.

The idea is simple. If someone publishes a tip under their own name, it can live here. One profile. One running points total. No deleted Saturdays.

Week one started with Frankie Monkhouse. The desk now takes anyone with a public byline and a price we can freeze. Score-only columns sit as notes so the board is full without pretending they are priced bets.

The rule for joining

A real name. A public page. A market and a price. We paraphrase the pick and link the source. We do not scrape anonymous Telegram sides or model dumps with no byline.
