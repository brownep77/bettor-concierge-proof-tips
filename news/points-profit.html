What points profit means

A point is one unit, not a pound. General price is the odds printed on the day.

Winner: stake × (decimal − 1). Loser: −stake. Void: 0. Each-way is split 50/50, default 1/5 odds three places.

We do not delete losers, back-fit prices, or count score predictions with no odds.
